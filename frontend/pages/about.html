Bio

I’m Manav-KM, a developer interested in creating software, websites, and technology projects that turn ideas into useful, real-world products. My work spans web development, applications, AI, automation, APIs, robotics, and hardware.

My strongest area is frontend development. I primarily work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a particular interest in interactive interfaces, responsive design, animation, and visual detail. I enjoy creating interfaces that feel complete and purposeful rather than simply making something that works.

My work also extends into the systems behind those interfaces. I work with Python, Firebase, Firestore, APIs, authentication, and AI integrations, continuing to develop backend architectures with technologies such as FastAPI and PostgreSQL.

Some of the software projects I’ve built include NexBoard (a classroom-oriented digital whiteboard application), Library, VelvetBites, AeroVent, Dnmx Central Command, and Dnmx Tactical Command. In robotics, my projects include CROWS (Crowd Risk Observation and Warning System) and an automatic fire-extinguishing vehicle.

I place a high value on security, clean engineering, building accessible free tools whenever possible, and delivering finished projects well ahead of deadlines.
