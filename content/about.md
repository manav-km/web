I'm Manav Kumar Mishra, a developer interested in creating software, websites, and technology projects that turn ideas into useful, real-world products. My work spans web development, applications, AI, automation, APIs, robotics, and hardware.

My strongest area is frontend development. I primarily work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a particular interest in interactive interfaces, responsive design, animation, and visual detail. I enjoy creating interfaces that feel complete and purposeful rather than simply making something that works.

My work also extends into the systems behind those interfaces. I work with Python, Firebase, Firestore, APIs, authentication, and AI integrations, and I'm continuing to develop my backend skills with technologies such as FastAPI and PostgreSQL. This has given me experience connecting different parts of an application and understanding how they work together as a complete system.

Some of the software projects I've worked on include NexBoard, a classroom-oriented digital whiteboard application; Library; VelvetBites; AeroVent; Dnmx Central Command; and Dnmx Tactical Command. Each project has explored different problems, technologies, and approaches to software development.

I'm particularly interested in AI and automation. Beyond using AI during development, I like exploring how AI can be integrated directly into applications to process information, automate repetitive work, and make software more capable.

Another major area of interest is robotics and physical computing. My projects include CROWS, an automatic fire-extinguishing car, and a handheld AI-powered farming station. These projects let me work with hardware, sensors, automation, and software while solving problems outside the traditional software environment.

I also have a strong preference for creating free-to-use applications whenever possible. I like building software that people can access without unnecessary barriers, especially when the project is intended to solve a practical problem or provide a useful tool.

Security is an important part of my development process as well. I pay attention to authentication, permissions, user data, APIs, and the ways an application can fail or be misused. As projects become more complex, I aim to make the underlying systems as responsible and secure as the interfaces themselves.

I place a high value on completing projects before their deadlines. I prefer to plan development around the final result, leaving enough time for implementation, testing, fixes, and final preparation rather than treating completion as something that happens at the very end.

I also enjoy learning through practical work. When a project requires a technology or concept I haven't used before, I'm willing to learn it and apply it directly rather than avoiding the challenge. That process has helped me gradually expand from frontend development into backend systems, AI, automation, databases, and robotics.

Outside of software, I'm interested in PC projects, hardware, robotics, and technology experimentation. I enjoy understanding how systems work and exploring what can be built by combining different areas of technology.

This portfolio brings together the projects, experiments, and ideas I've worked on so far, along with the things I continue to learn and build.
